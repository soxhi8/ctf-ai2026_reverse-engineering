### The vulnerability

- A plain line prints and advances.

- `CROWD (Singalong here!)` calls `input()` and **overwrites that same line** in the `song_lines` list with `'Crowd: ' + your_input`.
- A line matching `RETURN [0-9]+` jumps `lip` to that literal line index.
- Each line is split on `;` into sub-instructions first.
