        boulder_layout.append(random.randint(2, max_drill_level))

Whenever `i` matches the length of the string name of the current level (`len(LevelNames[current_level])`), a value of `-1` is appended. This means the safe column to drill straight down to find the bear corresponds exactly to the length of the level's name.

def CalculateScreenShake(sum):
